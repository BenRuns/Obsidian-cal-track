Cal Track

Look up a food, pick a quantity, unit and meal, and it's added to your food log. Each daily note shows that day's foods with live totals.

2026-10-06.md Example entries 

      
The note holds no food data, just a cal-track block. The plugin adds it the first time you log food for that day.

    

    

      
      
      
Every food you log is a row in Cal Track/log.csv. Edit it here and switch back to Preview: the note updates, as it does in Obsidian.

    

  


  
Tap a food to change its quantity, unit or meal, swap the food, or remove it.


  
Sample foods use approximate USDA values. The Obsidian plugin searches USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts live. Source: BenRuns/Obsidian-cal-track
